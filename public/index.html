STEP 01 / 04

Create a Telegram app

These credentials let the app connect to your account after you approve sign-in on your phone.

▣
1
Open the Telegram portal

Go to my.telegram.org ↗, sign in with your phone number, and enter the code sent to you in Telegram.

2
Choose API development tools

Create a new app. For App title, use a descriptive name such as My Research Collector. For Short name, use a short name without spaces, such as myresearchcollector.

3
Select a platform and copy your credentials

Choose Desktop because the connection uses a Telegram client. After creating the app, copy its App api_id and App api_hash exactly as shown.

Your credentials and session are encrypted by the server. Never share them or commit them to a public repository.

STEP 02 / 04

Connect with a QR code

Use Telegram on your phone to approve sign-in.

⌗
Preparing your QR code...
01

Open Telegram on your phone.

02

Go to Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device.

03

Scan the QR code and approve the connection.

Waiting for you to scan and approve...

⌁

One more step

Your account has a two-step verification password. Enter it to finish connecting. If you forgot it, open Telegram → Settings → Privacy and Security → Two-Step Verification and follow the recovery option available to your account.

✓

Connected successfully!

STEP 03 / 04

Set up your search

Choose your sources, dates, and the results you want to collect.

◫

Use a channel @username or a t.me link. Your account must have access to each source.

What should we collect?
Message filters All optional

Separate values with commas. One value may match within each field; if you fill several fields, a message must match all of them. Known stocks and countries also match common Arabic and English names.

You may get fewer results if there are not enough matches. Stock market mode shows the notebook's categories; choose “All text messages” for a broader search.
